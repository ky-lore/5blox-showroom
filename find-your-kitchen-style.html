About the showroom

The materials, first.

5blox is a Los Angeles showroom for the things a kitchen is actually made of — quarried stone, cabinetry from the makers that matter, and tile you won't find in a big box. We're appointment-only by design: fewer people, more attention, and the space to decide slowly.
